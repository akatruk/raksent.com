# Engagement and commercial readiness

## Service packages

| Package | Scope | Deliverables |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application and API assessment | Agreed applications, roles and environments | Executive and technical reports, prioritized remediation, optional retest |
| Cloud and infrastructure review | Agreed accounts, platforms, IAM, network and storage | Architecture review, findings register and hardening roadmap |
| DevSecOps review | Agreed repositories, build and artifact workflows | Pipeline and supply-chain review, policy backlog and implementation milestones |
| Monitoring readiness | Telemetry sources, escalation ownership and response | Coverage map, incident runbook and alert acceptance tests |
| Compliance readiness | Chosen standards, controls and evidence owners | Gap assessment, evidence register and plan for independent review |

## Statement of work

Record contracting entities, points of contact, objective, target inventory, desired assurance level, responsibilities, prerequisites, deliverables, acceptance criteria, schedule, retest allowance, compensation and change approval. Obtain signatures before onboarding. Agree on secure communications and handling of personal and regulated data.

## Rules of engagement

Record the owner and proof of authority for each asset. List approved source and destination boundaries, test accounts, environments, UTC windows, operational limits, incident contacts, escalation triggers, and who can stop work. Document explicitly excluded systems and dependencies, expected evidence and its storage location. Resolve ambiguity in writing before beginning. Keep an approval revision history. Do not collect client credentials or evidence through the public inquiry form.

## Workflow

1. Triage the inquiry, confirm the legal entity and asset ownership, and produce a signed scope.
2. Create an engagement and record the authorization reference in the private portal. Defer work until required approvals are recorded.
3. Ingest defensively collected or manually verified findings. Track asset or control identifier, affected service, observation time, severity, business impact, evidence reference, remediation owner and deadline. Record uncertainty and independent verification.
4. Send the executive summary and technical findings through the contracted secure channel. Assign a remediation owner and agree on acceptance or retest.
5. Verify corrections against the original observation and agreed scope. Record the result, UTC date, evidence reference and residual risk.
6. Close work after customer signoff. Execute the agreed retention plan across primary stores, evidence storage and backups.

## SLA terms to agree

Specify service and support windows, urgency definitions, target acknowledgment, communication cadence, restoration or handoff target, customer responsibilities, availability measurement, exclusions and escalation contacts. Set numbers only once staffing, data recovery and pager coverage have been tested.

## Data handling

Classify evidence before collection, minimize sensitive content and record reference IDs. Review operators and recipients against the engagement roster, encrypt in transit and at rest, and record access. Approve retention by data category and contract, including backup expiry and deletion verification. Restrict report downloads and revoke credentials at closeout.

Before taking client data, confirm the contracting entity and jurisdiction, privacy notice and required disclosures, customer agreements, authorization records, liability and insurance, processor terms, breach response, retention controls, and a legal review appropriate to the target market. Website copy and templates are operational drafts.
